# GuideOS 0.4.4.01 combined release

Date: 6 October 2026. Signed sequence 73, based on the returned Seed's installed
0.4.3.08 / sequence 72. The owner authorized repair, a combined build and a Seed
write. The root image is written, flushed and read back. The boot comparison
passed. At the owner's explicit request, the remaining full-data preservation
scan was stopped and verification standards were reduced for this hobby project.
Deck boot and physical acceptance remain pending.

## Included changes

- **Wi-Fi startup recovery:** preserve a working radio; after the exact current
  boot RTL8821CS initialization timeout, retry the same unbound SDIO device at
  most twice. Wait for the first probe to finish, preserve a still-bound device,
  and prevent repeated recovery after service restarts. NetworkManager waits for
  this bounded check. Missing-adapter discovery reports a specific result.
  [Diagnosis and limits](WIFI_COLD_BOOT_FAILURE_2026-10-06.md).
- **Video options:** Select opens exactly Subtitle track and Audio track. None
  is always a subtitle choice. D-pad navigates and A selects; B goes back or
  closes options. Menu input remains captured through close acknowledgement,
  preventing A/B from also pausing or leaving the video. Decoder track identities
  and selected-state acknowledgements are verified.
  [Control contract](VIDEO_OPTIONS_TRACKS_0.md).
- **NDI 1.0.4:** the Windows executable is rebuilt. Automatic FFmpeg preparation
  retains up to eight AAC audio tracks with language/title metadata and supported
  text subtitles. Existing single-track cache entries are not reused as new
  multi-track output. Bitmap subtitles and alternative-track preservation through
  manual/VLC fallbacks remain unsupported or unverified.
- **Planegotchi backend:** matching continuous world host, lifecycle, protocol,
  worker, ARM64 Supervisor, committed event feed/acknowledgement and conserved
  society-founding jobs from the verified backend handoff.
- **Approved grid presentation:** five 32-pixel cell maps and five visual society
  profiles, used-art package, complete 884-recipe cold library and tradition
  registry. Cell navigation, edge panning, plot pointing, information pages and
  portrait rendering accompany the versioned presentation.
- **Combined baseline:** pending viewer/compact-plan changes, browser removal,
  Notepad, Quick Find, storage and exactly one previous rollback. Installed
  inventory, policy and native installation metadata are regenerated together.

The five visual societies do not migrate the simulation fixture or the owner's
private world. Active art-review and unrelated dirty work are preserved outside
the frozen release scope. Design document edits are not reset.

## Card and physical acceptance

The writer matched the connected card against the fresh full-region hashes,
wrote only the bounded root partition, flushed, reopened the card and compared
the entire root and boot hashes. It then entered its final full-data scan. The
owner requested lighter checks, noting that most of the card is empty, and that
remaining scan was stopped. `installation.json` records
`GUIDE_0_4_4_01_ROOT_READBACK_VERIFIED_OWNER_DATA_SCAN_SKIPPED`;
`bootDataAndTailUnchanged` is null because the complete post-write data check
was not finished. The completed-write transcript, observed transfer counts and
stop record are retained. No writer remains running.

Future checks should be focused and proportionate. Do not default to reading all
unused card space or rerunning broad suites when physical Deck testing will
shortly establish the actual behavior.

Needed Deck checks are repeated full-power-off boots with working Wi-Fi interface,
network discovery and connection; Select options during running/paused video;
None and real subtitle rendering/readability; alternative audio and synchronization;
D-pad/A/B isolation and global exit; approved grid presentation/navigation and
continued saved-world state. No physical pass has been claimed.
