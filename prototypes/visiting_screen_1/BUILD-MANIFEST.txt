Automated tests: 9 passed

Verified behaviors:

* Both standalone executables start and communicate on the local network.
* The Deck retains the selected file and registers a temporary stream offer.
* The host displays the Deck offer without receiving a full movie copy.
* Pairing codes expire and rotate after one accepted offer or upload.
* Mock TV receives SetAVTransportURI followed by Play.
* Whole-file and byte-range media responses support playback and seeking.
* Owner revocation immediately disables the Deck stream.
* Deck streams automatically expire after six hours.
* The optional host-upload fallback deletes its temporary copy on revocation.

Included instructions:

* OWNER-INSTRUCTIONS.txt for the person controlling the visited network.
* DECK-INSTRUCTIONS.txt for the visitor offering the movie.
* FIELD-TEST-CHECKLIST.txt for recording the controlled test.

Not yet verified:

* Windows versions other than the build computer's Windows 10 environment.
* Networks with client isolation, VLAN boundaries, or disabled multicast.
* Video decoding compatibility on the destination TV.
