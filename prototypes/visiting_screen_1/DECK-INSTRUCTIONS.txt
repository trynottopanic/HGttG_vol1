THIRD WAY DECK MEDIA 0.1.0 - VISITOR INSTRUCTIONS
==================================================

Use this experiment only with the informed permission of the person who
controls the destination network and television. Use a movie you are legally
permitted to show.

1. Connect the Deck computer to the owner's approved local network.
2. Ask the owner to start Third Way Temporary Node and give you the displayed Visitor
   pairing address. The address works once and expires after 15 minutes.
3. Double-click ThirdWayDeck.exe.
4. Drag the movie file into the black Deck window, then press Enter.
5. Paste the complete Visitor pairing address, then press Enter.
6. Keep the Deck computer awake, connected, and the program open while the
   owner reviews the offer and controls playback.
7. Press Enter in the Deck window whenever you want to withdraw your offer.

The movie remains on the Deck. The owner's host receives its filename, size,
media type, and temporary local addresses, but does not receive a full copy in
this primary workflow. The destination TV reads the temporary stream directly
from the Deck.

The owner can stop playback and revoke the stream from the host at any time.
Access also ends when you press Enter, close the Deck program, disconnect from
the network, or reach the six-hour time limit.

CURRENT LIMITS

* These unsigned experimental programs may trigger a Windows warning.
* Local traffic is not yet encrypted.
* Only TVs advertising UPnP/DLNA AVTransport can be selected automatically.
* The Deck does not yet convert video into a format the TV supports.
* A TV may be discovered and still reject an incompatible movie.

If the host rejects the pairing address or cannot find the Deck, confirm that
both computers are on the same owner-approved private network. Do not weaken
the network's security settings to force the test.
