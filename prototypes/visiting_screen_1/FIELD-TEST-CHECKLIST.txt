VISITING-SCREEN-1 FIELD TEST
============================

BEFORE LEAVING

[ ] Use a short video you created, a public-domain video, or another video you
    are clearly authorized to show in the store.
[ ] Prefer an MP4 containing H.264 video and AAC audio for the first attempt.
[ ] Take the ThirdWay VISITING-SCREEN-1 ZIP and its SHA-256 checksum.
[ ] Take the Deck computer, its charger, and the authorized test video.
[ ] Do not assume the store owner will agree or has authority over the TV and
    network. Accept a refusal immediately.

WITH THE OWNER

[ ] Explain that this is unsigned experimental software and local network
    discovery, not a commercial product.
[ ] Confirm that the owner controls the Windows computer, network, and TV.
[ ] Let the owner read OWNER-INSTRUCTIONS.txt before running anything.
[ ] Read DECK-INSTRUCTIONS.txt before offering the movie.
[ ] Have the owner run the installer, read its explanation, and start Third Way
    Temporary Node themselves.
[ ] If prompted, the owner allows only the network profile they control.
[ ] Connect the Deck computer to the same owner-approved local network.
[ ] Run ThirdWayDeck.exe, choose the test video, and paste the Visitor pairing
    address shown on the owner's screen.
[ ] Keep the Deck program open. Confirm the owner sees the filename and size;
    the full movie should not be transferred to the owner's host.
[ ] Let the owner verify the filename and size, search for TVs, select the
    intended TV, and press Play.

RECORD THE RESULT

[ ] Did the intended TV appear by its correct name and model?
[ ] Did the TV accept the playback request?
[ ] Did video appear?
[ ] Did audio play?
[ ] Did seeking work?
[ ] Did Stop and revoke end playback?
[ ] Was the movie unavailable after revocation?
[ ] Did the Deck report or behave as revoked after the owner stopped access?

INTERPRETING FAILURE

No TVs found:
The network may block multicast or isolate clients, the TV may be on another
network segment, or the TV may not advertise UPnP/DLNA AVTransport.

TV found but request rejected:
The TV may require a vendor-specific protocol or additional authorization.

Playback accepted but no usable picture or sound:
The TV probably cannot decode the video's container, video codec, audio codec,
resolution, or bitrate. The prototype does not yet transcode.

FINISH

[ ] The owner presses Stop and revoke.
[ ] The owner closes the host window.
[ ] The owner deletes the extracted prototype if they do not want to keep it.
[ ] Do not troubleshoot by weakening the store's network security.
